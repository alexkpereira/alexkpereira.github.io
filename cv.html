Initiatives

Societies

2022 – present
Co-founder of PRISM: Promoting the Interdisciplinary Study of Mental health and illness (with Carly Frieders).
PRISM (Promoting the Interdisciplinary Study of Mental Health and Illness) is a reading group and research community uniting Stanford scholars from diverse disciplines to explore foundational questions about mental health and well-being. Despite decades of research, the causes and nature of mental disorders remain elusive, highlighting the need for interdisciplinary integration across sciences and humanities. Mental health is studied from medicine, cognitive science, law, policy, anthropology, and philosophy, yet no forum existed for these perspectives to meet. PRISM convenes fortnightly over dinner to discuss participant-suggested readings on questions such as: What counts as a mental disorder and who decides? How do norms shape psychiatric classification? Why has biological psychiatry struggled to deliver breakthroughs? Are AI therapists ethical and effective? / Link
