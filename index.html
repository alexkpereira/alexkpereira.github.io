Alexander Pereira

PhD candidate in Philosophy and Psychology, Stanford University

I work in the philosophy of science, medicine, and psychiatry. My research concerns how mental disorders are explained, classified, and understood. I am particularly interested in emotion, fear, and anxiety, in the regulatory systems involved in them, and in how biology and culture interact in mental illness. I am affiliated with the Stanford Psychophysiology Laboratory and the Stanford Center for Affective Science.

Before Stanford I completed a BSc (First Class Honours) and an MPhil at the University of Sydney, where my studies spanned anatomy, neuroscience, psychology, and philosophy. I am originally from Sydney and now live in San Francisco.

You can reach me at pereirak [at] stanford [dot] edu.

Research

I am interested in what it takes for something to count as a disorder, and in how regulation and control figure in psychiatric explanation.

Areas: philosophy of psychiatry; philosophy of science and medicine; emotion, fear, and anxiety; the nature of dysfunction and disorder; regulation and control.

Teaching

I have taught and assisted across the University of Sydney and Stanford for close to a decade, in philosophy and psychology.
